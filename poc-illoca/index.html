An Open Letter

We did not build this for the people who want fewer architects. We built it for the ones who still keep a pencil on the desk and a plan on the wall — because the drawing is where the thinking happens, and the thinking is the job.

Every tool before this one asked you to translate. Sketch to model, model to plan, plan back to sketch. The translation cost more than the design. So we removed it.

What is left is a table, a sheet of tracing paper, and something that keeps up with you.

— the illoca studio

FAQs · need help?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is illoca a CAD replacement?
No. It sits in front of your CAD, at the stage where the drawing is still an argument. Everything leaves as IFC, DWG or a plain SVG sheet.
What happens to my drawings?
They stay yours, and they stay out of training runs. Studio plans can pin the model to their own tenancy.
Does it run on a laptop?
The canvas is local and cheap; the heavy passes run remotely. A four year old machine is fine.
Can I keep drawing by hand?
That is the entire point. Pen input is the first-class input; the prompt bar is the fallback, not the other way round.
How do the credits work?
One credit per generated variant. Sketch, edit and export cost nothing — you only pay when the machine proposes something.
