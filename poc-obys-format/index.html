(Format) is a decision you make first

A format is chosen before there is anything to say. Everything that follows — the picture, the sentence, the pause — happens inside it. This sheet is a working note about that decision, and it is built the way it argues: one typeface, one weight, one variable.

Every measure on this page hangs on --s, and --s is nothing but the viewport divided by 1440. Resize the window and the sheet does not respond — it scales. The readout at the bottom shows what that costs.
