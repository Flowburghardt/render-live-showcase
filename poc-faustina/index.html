who I am

about

Fifteen years of posters, identities and campaigns out of a third-floor room in Brooklyn. One display face, one acid yellow, no rounded corners — the constraint is the method, not the mood board.

I work directly with founders and in-house teams, usually from the first sketch to the printed sheet. Small studio, long engagements, very few clients at a time.
